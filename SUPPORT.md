# Getting help

KYM is an experimental, single-maintainer proof of concept; support is best effort.

* **Questions about what KYM is or is not:** read the [FAQ](docs/faq.md), [glossary](docs/glossary.md) and [philosophy](docs/philosophy/README.md).
* **How do I run or use it?** [Getting started](docs/getting-started.md), [examples](examples/README.md), [README](README.md#run-it).
* **Something is broken:** open a [bug report](https://github.com/PaulClas/Know-The-Mandate/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) with the output of `make test` for the failing area.
* **Idea or critique:** open a [feature request](https://github.com/PaulClas/Know-The-Mandate/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml). Critiques of the design are explicitly welcome; the best form is a failing attack in `redteam.py`.
* **Security vulnerability:** do not open a public issue; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
* **Legal or regulatory questions:** KYM gives no legal advice. Consult counsel.
